Financial and legal articles fall strictly under Google's YMYL (*Your Money or Your Life*) evaluation criteria. When readers in Poland look up PIT tax thresholds, ZUS health contribution rules for sole proprietors (JDG), or household electricity tariffs approved by the Energy Regulatory Office ([URE](https://www.ure.gov.pl/)), vague paragraphs cause immediate bounces.

## Key Structural Rules From Our Newsroom

1. **Primary Legal Citations:** Every tax or pension table links directly to [ISAP Sejm RP](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/), [Podatki.gov.pl](https://www.podatki.gov.pl/), and [ZUS.pl](https://www.zus.pl/).
2. **Gross-to-Net Comparison Columns:** Always show gross amount (*brutto*), tax/health deduction, and exact net payout (*na reke*).
